About Me

I am a 4th-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student with a deep passion for 3D art and digital design. My journey into 3D began with a fascination for video games and animated films, which led me to explore the world of 3D modeling using Blender.

As a motivated and creative aspiring 3D Artist, I possess skills in 3D modeling, basic texturing, and creating 3D assets. I have a strong interest in digital art, visual design, and 3D animation. I am constantly seeking to learn new techniques and improve my technical skills to bring imaginative concepts to life. My IT background provides me with a logical and problem-solving approach to the creative process.
